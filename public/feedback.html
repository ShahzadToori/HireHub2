Common questions
How do I remove my job listing?
Contact us with the job title and company name and we will remove it within 24 hours.
Can I edit a listing after posting?
Yes. Send us the updated details and we will update your listing at no charge.
How long do listings stay live?
Standard listings stay active for 30 days. Featured and Sponsored listings are renewed monthly.
