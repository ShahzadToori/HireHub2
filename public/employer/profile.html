Write your own format once — it auto-fills when sharing jobs on WhatsApp.

Placeholders are words in {curly brackets} that get replaced with real data when you share a job.

AVAILABLE PLACEHOLDERS:

{company} → Your company name {title} → Job title {location} → City/region {type} → Full-time, Contract… {salary} → Salary range {description} → Job description {phone} → Phone number {whatsapp} → WhatsApp number {link} → Job URL {positions} → Total openings (bulk) {titles} → Job list (bulk)

EXAMPLE:

📋 *{title}*
📍 {location} | 💰 {salary}

🔗 {link}

Unused placeholders (e.g. empty salary) are removed automatically.

Tap placeholder to copy: {company} {title} {location} {type} {salary} {description} {phone} {whatsapp} {link} {positions} {titles}

Extra placeholders: {positions} (total count), {titles} (bullet list)
