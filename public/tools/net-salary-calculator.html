Monthly Take-Home Pay
Saudi Arabia has no income tax. The only deduction is GOSI (social insurance). Expatriates pay 2% on basic salary. Saudi nationals pay 10% (employee share).

Understanding GOSI Deductions

GOSI (General Organization for Social Insurance) is Saudi Arabia's social security system. Contributions are based on basic salary only — not allowances.
