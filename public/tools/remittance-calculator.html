Best Ways to Send Money from Saudi Arabia

Millions of expat workers remit money from Saudi Arabia each month. Here are the most popular options:

  • Bank transfer: Most Saudi banks offer remittance. Al-Rajhi Bank is popular for transfers to South Asia and Southeast Asia.
  • Wise (TransferWise): Best exchange rates, low fees. Works well for transfers to Europe, US, Philippines.
  • Western Union / MoneyGram: Available everywhere, good for recipients without bank accounts.
  • STC Pay / Urpay: Popular Saudi fintech apps for instant transfers to Pakistan, India, Egypt.
  • Remitly: Fast transfers with good rates for South Asia.

Tip: Compare rates before every transfer. A difference of 0.5% on SAR 5,000 = SAR 25 lost per transfer, or SAR 300 per year.
