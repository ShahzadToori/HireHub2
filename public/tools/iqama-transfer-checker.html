How Iqama Transfer Works in 2026

Saudi Arabia's updated Labour Law made iqama transfers significantly easier for expat workers. Here is what you need to know:

  • After 1 year: You can transfer without your employer's consent if they are Platinum or Green rated.
  • Red-rated employer: You can transfer at any time, even before 1 year, because the employer is non-compliant.
  • Expired iqama: Must be renewed before any transfer can be processed through Absher.
  • Process: New employer initiates the transfer through the Absher platform (absher.sa). You do not visit any office.
  • Cost: Transfer fees apply (typically SAR 200–500). Your new employer usually covers these.
