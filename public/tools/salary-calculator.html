Understanding Gulf Salary Packages

Gulf salaries typically consist of a basic salary plus allowances. Most common package components:

  • Basic Salary: 60–70% of total package. Used to calculate EOSB and overtime.
  • Housing Allowance: Typically 25–30% of basic salary.
  • Transport Allowance: SAR 500–2,000/month depending on role.
  • Annual Flight Ticket: One return flight to home country.
  • Medical Insurance: For employee and often family.
